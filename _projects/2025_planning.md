**Abstract** This talk dicusses the current challenges and solultions to improving LLM reasoning and planning abilities, covering three recent work, **AgentBoard** -- benchmarking agentic planning, **Predictive-Decoding** -- understanding cause of deficiencies and solving with Test-Time Scaling, and **Genius** -- RL training framework.
