Bio

I am a postdoc at The University of Washington, co-advised by Pang Wei Koh and Hanna Hajishirzi. I completed my Ph.D at The University of Hong Kong where I was fortunately co-advised by Lingpeng Kong and Tao Yu, and also worked closely with Junxian He. Before that, I received my B.S. in Computer Science from Peking University, advised by Zhihong Deng.

My research goal is to develop Autonomous, Efficient and Trustworthy Agents, with a long-term focus on advancing scientific discoveries. -->
